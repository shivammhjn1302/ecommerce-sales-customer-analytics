# Business Insights Report

## Executive Findings
- Total revenue analyzed: ₹1,219,094,880 across 58,000 transactions and 12,000 customers.
- Portfolio profit margin: 18.0%; average order value: ₹21,019.
- Top 20% customers contribute 74.0% of customer revenue, confirming a strong VIP retention opportunity.
- Discounted orders contribute 85.3% of orders but run at 18.0% margin versus 18.5% for non-discounted orders.
- Highest-return category: Fashion with 9.9% return rate.
- Repeat customers show materially higher AOV: ₹20,672 vs ₹19,812 for one-time buyers.

## Recommended Actions
1. Launch VIP lifecycle journeys for Champions and high monetary K-Means clusters.
2. Audit sizing/quality pages for high-return Fashion and Electronics SKUs.
3. Shift blanket discounts into category-specific margin-safe offers.
4. Prioritize delivery SLA fixes in regions with high shipping delay percentages.
5. Use churn scores to trigger win-back campaigns before 120 days of inactivity.
